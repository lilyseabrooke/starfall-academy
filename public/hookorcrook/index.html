By Hook or By Crook

New Deputy Enforcer’s been making waves. Shadows are saying he’s got secrets that might change that, though.

✦

Now, for the uninitiated, Deputy Enforcer’s a cursed position. Nobody ever makes it more than a year. Pretty bureaucratic post—generally a changing of the guard’s a flicker you don’t feel. A scandal happens somewhere out of view. New one comes in. Black-suits grumble and make small talk, and it settles in until something gets the new one taken out.

This time around’s a whole other field of magic.

✦

Meet Samuel Lewis Wayne, Mayweather alum. Studied summoning back in the SIEF incident in Mayweather and then hung up his golden bolts to work with artifacts ever since. Before coming here, he was an FDM spook for eight years, with multiple high-level ops in Central American magic facilities.

He’s good with his wand and a menace in the artificy lab, but infinitely more dangerous, he’s got a knack for working a crowd. He’s been more public-facing than past Deputy Enforcers, delivering regular State of Starfall briefings, positioning himself as a demagogue while Head Enforcer Walker is the staid old dragon at the head of things.

People love him. That’s what makes him dangerous.

✦

He’s a crony enabler, selling off backdoor access to the Garrison and shelling Academy artifacts for secrets to auction off. His son Daniel Lewis Wayne got a powerful administrative position off the credentials of his father’s name alone, when little Daniel wouldn’t know a spell if it hit him in the face.

And Samuel isn’t subtle with his ambitions—even though he never says it outright, everyone knows he wants to be Head Enforcer, and a dangerous and growing number of people want him there.

✦

His issues go beyond the professional. He’s known for being a cheating womanizer, for leveraging his professional power to use against his personal opponents and for petty grievances, and the FDM is scary quiet about the day-to-day reality of his operations in Central America, so all we know is that this man’s net worth went up by no small sum over those years.

Suffice to say, he’s made his fair share of enemies. One of which has come to you.

✦

A mastermind with her identity obscured, who goes only by her moniker, Riot. She won’t say what her issue with him is—only that there is one, in that tone of voice that says it’s no surprise that there is.

And Riot knows a very dangerous secret about this man. A secret she wants your help to exploit.

✦

On December 14th, Samuel is hosting a symposium for powerful heritage magic interests. To democratize heritage magic and bring the interests closer to the people.

And also to bring his high-paying heritage magic cronies closer to what might be Starfall’s most tightly controlled legendary artifact. Saints only know what they’re paying him. And saints only know what they’re planning to do with it at that symposium that they’re willing to pay him as much as they did.

Starfall keeps one of the world’s largest repositories of ancient and legendary artifacts. There’s plenty under lock and rune, but most of them pale next to the Shepherd’s Crook.

You wouldn’t think it looking at the thing. Looks like a classic wooden cane, sheep’s head carved at the top. But even looking at the thing can be enough to bring a lesser caster to their knees.

The Shepherd’s Crook belonged to an ancient true demon, the Shepherd of the Forsaken, for a thousand years before it fell into human hands. Its full capabilities are yet undiscovered, because of the amount of restriction that goes into a true demon’s legendary artifact, but it’s said to be able to steer the direction of a person’s life.

✦

And Samuel Lewis Wayne is keeping it secretly at this symposium of his, letting someone or someones do something with it, behind the Academy’s back.

✦

Riot’s request is simple.

Obtain the Shepherd’s Crook, and bring it to her instead.

The reward—well, at risk of sounding like a cliché, you’ll never need to work a day in your lives.

And Samuel Lewis Wayne alone will be left holding the bag.

✦

There’s no doubt the mission is suicide. Samuel has incredible resources, and he’s going to be deploying everything he has to guard this. He wouldn’t leave a single loose thread: no way in, and no way out.

But every Starfaller wants to be part of a legend.

And this is your ticket.

✦ By Hook or By Crook
